# branch-mass

From the equation:

$$\frac{M_d}{C_d} = 1$$

---

### Step 1: Multiply both sides by $C_d$

To isolate $M_d$, multiply both sides of the equation by $C_d$ (assuming $C_d \neq 0$):

$$M_d = C_d$$

---

### Summary of Implications

* **Equivalence:** The quantities $M_d$ and $C_d$ are strictly equal ($M_d = C_d$).
* **Constraint:** $C_d \neq 0$ (otherwise the ratio is undefined).
* **Difference:** $M_d - C_d = 0$.
